# Claim audit: citations in research/01, 02, 03

Audit run 2026-10-31. Method: WebFetch only (Europe PMC REST, PubMed/PMC, ERIC, Semantic Scholar, Unpaywall, institutional repositories, web search). No downloads, no pirated copies, no paywall workarounds. Result in one paragraph: of the ten priority claims, only Katahira et al. 2020 (the 42% figure) could be checked against an open-access full text. Five more (McCrae & Costa 1989, Arnau et al. 2003, Bess & Harvey 2002, Randall et al. 2017, Capraro & Capraro 2002) were checked against abstracts or abstract-level records only, and every number I could see there matches the repo. Pittenger 1993/2005, Harvey 1996, Reynierse 2009, Reynierse & Harker 2008, Furnham 1996 and Erford et al. 2025 could not be reached in any legal open form. No claim was found Wrong, so no numbers were changed in files 01-03 and no "abstract only" label was upgraded. Open-access routes mostly failed: Wiley, SAGE, APA, Elsevier and T&F versions are closed (Semantic Scholar lists Capraro as CLOSED; Europe PMC flags Bess & Harvey and McCrae & Costa as not open access), and the Randall PDF on gwern.net is image-encoded and unreadable by the fetch tool.

## Net effect on the repo

- No Wrong items. No numbers in files 01, 02 or 03 contradicted by a primary source I read.
- No "abstract only" labels upgraded (no new full texts read; Katahira was already marked full text).
- Still unverified and still best treated as secondary: Capraro & Capraro numbers, McCrae & Costa and Furnham coefficients, Pittenger and Harvey retest figures, Reynierse "1 of 540", Randall T-F .61, Erford details.
- Suggested follow-up for someone with library access or author copies: Pittenger 1993 (Review of Educational Research 63(4)), Harvey 1996 chapter, Reynierse 2009 in the Journal of Psychological Type, and the McCrae & Costa 1989 correlation table.
