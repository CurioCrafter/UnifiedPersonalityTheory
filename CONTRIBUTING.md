# Contributing

Everyone is welcome: researchers, students, people who love type theory, people who think it is nonsense. You do not need credentials. You need sources and a willingness to be proven wrong.

## Ways to help

- **Check a claim.** Pick any graded claim in `research/`, read its source, and open an issue or pull request if the grade or number is wrong. This is the most useful thing you can do.
- **Run a test.** Rerun a script in `analysis/` on the data it names, or pick up an open hypothesis in [HYPOTHESES.md](HYPOTHESES.md).
- **Add a hypothesis.** It must say what result would count against it.
- **Add a research strand.** Copy the structure of an existing file in `research/`.

## The rules for evidence

1. **Read what you cite.** Mark every source "full text read" or "abstract only". A citation you did not open goes under "Could not verify".
2. **Give the number.** Effect sizes and sample sizes, where the source states them.
3. **Say who paid.** Flag studies funded or published by a test publisher.
4. **Grade the claim:** `[Strong]` replicated across independent samples, `[Moderate]`, `[Weak]` single study or publisher-only, `[Contested]` credible sources disagree, `[Untested]` no empirical test found.
5. **Fix the test before seeing the result.** Commit the hypothesis and its decision rule before running the analysis. If you change either afterwards, say so.
6. **Report null and unwelcome results.** They count the same as any other.

## Using AI agents

Agent-assisted work is welcome and expected here, under these rules:

- **A person is responsible for every pull request.** Say which tool you used and which parts it did.
- **Agents must open each source they cite.** Language models invent plausible citations. Check every DOI or URL before submitting.
- **Model output is never human evidence.** Simulated survey respondents and model-guessed types can suggest hypotheses. They cannot support one.

## Do not add

- Items or scoring keys from the official MBTI or any other proprietary test.
- Text under a licence incompatible with ours. OEJTS items, for example, are CC BY-NC-SA, so link to them.
- Personal data. Published data must be anonymised and its terms must allow redistribution. Otherwise link to it and let the script fetch it.
- Typing of named private individuals.
- Advice to use type for hiring, diagnosis or decisions about a person.
- Data you collected from people without informed consent and, where your institution or country requires it, ethics review.

## Licensing of contributions

By submitting a contribution you agree it is released under the repo's licences: [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE) for research and writing, [MIT](LICENSE-CODE) for code. You keep your copyright. You must have the right to submit it. Those terms keep the research free: anyone may reuse or build on it, even commercially, as long as they credit it and share adapted writing under the same licence.

## Conduct

Argue with claims and leave people out of it. Harassment, including mocking someone's type or beliefs about type, gets contributions closed.
